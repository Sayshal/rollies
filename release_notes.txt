## Bug Fixes

- Close buttons on the player roll, bracket tournament, GM tie notification, and winner announcement dialogs now close the dialog.
- Closing a player roll or bracket dialog by any means now stops its countdown and has the GM auto-roll for that player.
- Players using manual dice no longer get a second roll when their roll-off or bracket match times out.
- The dialog countdown now restarts when the manual dice warning period begins.
- The GM now waits through the full manual dice grace period before auto-rolling for a player.

## Changes

- Dialog buttons now use the standard Foundry footer style.
