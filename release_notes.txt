## New Features

- A chat card lists the final initiative order after each rolloff.
- A tied match shows the tied roll before it is re-rolled.
- Brackets take any number of tied combatants, seeded by highest Dexterity.
- Clicking the minimized bar reopens the overlay.
- Deleting the combat mid-rolloff closes the overlay.
- Each tied initiative value runs its own rolloff, one after another.
- Every client sees the overlay during a rolloff.
- Every match in a bracket round rolls at the same time.
- Every tied combatant gets a distinct initiative from its finishing place.
- Hidden combatants show as unknown to players.
- Losers in the same bracket round rank by their losing roll.
- Losers with equal losing rolls in the same round are ordered by an automatic roll-off.
- Players roll from a Roll button in their combatant's bracket slot.
- Roll results wait for Dice So Nice dice to settle.
- Rolloff chat cards are whispered to GMs when a hidden combatant is involved.
- The bracket, player roll and winner announcement dialogs are replaced by one full-screen knockout overlay.
- The initiative order fills in along the bottom of the overlay as rounds finish.
- The overlay can be closed once the winner is decided.
- The overlay can be minimized to a small bar at the top of the screen while the rolloff runs.
- The overlay shows which player each pending roll is waiting on.
- Top seeds in the bracket receive byes.
- Two-way ties use a head-to-head showdown layout.

## Bug Fixes

- A rolloff no longer stalls when a player never answers a roll request.

## Changes

- The Show Winner Announcements setting is renamed to Hold on the Winner and keeps the bracket on screen for a few seconds after the winner is decided.
